# Code of Conduct

This project should be a practical, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and bugs.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately introducing unsafe automation, credential leaks, or malicious code.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may lead to being blocked from participating in the project.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately. For security-sensitive reports, follow `SECURITY.md`.
